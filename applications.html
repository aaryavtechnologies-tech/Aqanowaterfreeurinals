Applications & Where to Use

Customized scale prevention and water quality enhancement across residential, commercial, industrial, and agricultural sectors.

1. Housing Societies & Borewells 2. Flats & Bungalows 3. Factories & Industries 4. Agriculture & Farming
Category 01

Housing Societies & Common Borewells

Managing hard borewell water in high-rise residential towers and housing societies is a continuous headache for management committees. Conventional salt softeners require 500kg to 2,000kg of salt every month, dedicated operators, and frequent motor breakdowns.

With AquaPro Society Solutions, a single heavy-duty inline unit (2", 3", or 4" flanged) is installed on the central borewell delivery line or overhead storage tank distribution riser.

  • Zero Monthly Salt Expense: Saves societies ₹25,000 to ₹75,000+ each month in bulk salt purchases.
  • Protects Common Risers: Prevents vertical multi-storey plumbing lines from getting calcified and choked.
  • Happy Residents: Eliminates resident complaints about salt water, slippery bath feel, or geyser coil burnouts.
  • No Operator Required: Completely automatic operation that functions whenever the water pump runs.
Category 02

Flats, Villas & Independent Bungalows

Homeowners invest lakhs in designer bathrooms, Italian tiles, Grohe/Kohler/Jaquar CP fittings, and digital glass shower cabins—only to see them ruined by stubborn white calcium stains within months.

Our compact 3/4" or 1" domestic models connect directly to the flat’s individual water inlet shaft or the rooftop terrace line of independent bungalows.

  • Hair Fall & Skin Wellness: Relieves scalp dryness, itching, and hair thinning caused by calcium mineral deposits.
  • Protects Appliances: Doubles the working life of washing machines, dishwashers, solar heaters, and storage geysers.
  • Spotless Bathrooms: Chrome taps, shower heads, and glass doors stay spotless with effortless wiping.
  • Zero Water Wastage: Unlike RO softeners, 100% of the water passing through the conditioner is utilized.
Category 03

Factories, Boilers & Cooling Towers

In industrial manufacturing plants, scale is the #1 enemy of thermal efficiency. A mere 1.5mm calcium carbonate deposit on heat exchanger tubes or boiler plates acts as thermal insulation, forcing boilers to burn 15% to 25% more fuel.

AquaPro Heavy Industrial Conditioners handle high-volume flow rates up to 1,80,000+ LPH. Engineered with heavy-duty stainless steel, they maintain maximum heat transfer coefficient without acid washing shutdowns.

  • Cooling Towers & Chillers: Prevents condenser scale build-up, maintains high Delta-T, and lowers compressor head pressure.
  • Boiler Feedwater Pre-Treatment: Drastically decreases boiler blowdown cycles and prevents tube ruptures.
  • RO Plant Membrane Protection: Prolongs membrane life by preventing calcium carbonate fouling.
  • Food, Pharma & Textile Processing: Clean, non-corrosive water quality meeting demanding process specifications.
Category 04

Agriculture, Farms & Drip Irrigation

Agricultural borewells across Gujarat frequently deliver high-TDS, highly alkaline water that chokes precision drip emitters, creates white saline crusting on topsoil, and stunts crop root growth.

Installed directly onto the submersible pump outlet pipe, the AquaPro Agricultural Conditioner treats high-pressure irrigation flows without electricity or maintenance.

  • Zero Clogged Drip Nozzles: Aragonite micro-spheres pass right through micro-emitters and sprinkler heads without depositing scale.
  • Improves Soil Water Infiltration: Lowers surface tension of water, allowing deeper moisture penetration into root zones.
  • Breaks Hardpan Topsoil: Prevents impervious white calcium crusting on farm soil, improving aeration and fertilizer uptake.
  • Enhanced Crop Health & Yield: Plants absorb moisture and nutrients more effectively, resulting in healthier produce.
