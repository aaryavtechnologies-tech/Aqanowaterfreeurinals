Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before arranging your water conditioner installation.

How quickly can the unit be delivered and installed in Ahmedabad?
Standard residential models (1/2", 3/4", 1") and common housing society models (1.5", 2") are readily in stock for Ahmedabad and surrounding regions. Deliveries and plumber installations can typically be completed within 24 to 48 hours of confirmation.
Is a site visit available before purchase?
Yes. For housing societies, commercial properties, factories, and agricultural farms, Mukeshbhai Thakkar can schedule a site visit in Ahmedabad to inspect the pipeline layout, water pump capacity, and advise the best installation point.
Do you offer product demonstrations?
Yes! You can visit our live demonstration facility at A/1501, Riviera Springs, Skycity, Club O7 Road, Shela, Ahmedabad. You can also bring a sample of your borewell water to test its hardness and observe the transformation.
What payment and enquiry options are available?
We accept bank NEFT/RTGS, UPI, cheques, and online payment transfers. Invoices and warranty certificates are issued upon delivery. You can initiate an enquiry directly via phone or WhatsApp at +91 79845 15685.
