Health & Wellness

Healthy Skin, Silky Hair & Soft Fabrics

When bathing in untreated hard water, soap reacts with calcium ions to form an insoluble residue (soap scum) that coats your scalp, clogs pores, causes chronic dandruff, and leads to severe hair thinning.

Conditioned water allows soaps and shampoos to lather richly and rinse cleanly away. Your skin retains its natural moisture barrier without needing excessive lotions, and hair regains its natural shine and bounce.

  • Significantly reduces post-shower hair fall and scalp dryness
  • Clothes come out of the washing machine softer and brighter
  • Uses 30% less detergent, shampoo, and bath soap
Asset Protection

Protecting Appliances & Concealed Plumbing

Replacing concealed bathroom plumbing pipes damaged by lime encrustation can cost upwards of ₹1,00,000 to ₹3,00,000 in demolition and masonry repairs.

The AquaPro conditioner acts as an impenetrable shield for your entire plumbing infrastructure, geysers, dishwashers, solar heaters, and RO drinking water plants.

  • Doubles the operational lifespan of electric & solar geysers
  • Protects expensive RO membranes from fouling and premature replacement
  • Maintains full water pressure and flow across multi-storey risers
