Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you understand how water conditioning works for your property.

How is a Water Conditioner different from a traditional Water Softener?
Traditional water softeners use chemical ion-exchange resins that swap healthy calcium and magnesium minerals for sodium (salt). They require constant 25kg salt bag replenishment, continuous electrical supply, and dump hundreds of litres of salty backwash down the drain. The AquaPro Water Conditioner is a 100% physical catalytic device: it alters the crystal structure of calcium from sticky Calcite to non-stick Aragonite without removing essential minerals, without using salt, and with zero water waste.
Will it reduce the TDS of my borewell water?
No. A water conditioner does not reduce TDS because it does not remove minerals from the water. Instead, it conditions the hard minerals so they cannot crystallize and stick to your pipes, geysers, taps, or skin. Only reverse osmosis (RO) systems reduce TDS (which is necessary for drinking water, but wasteful and corrosive for whole-house utility lines).
Where is the product installed?
It is installed inline on your main water inlet pipe: either directly after the borewell pump discharge, after the municipal meter, or on the outlet line from the overhead storage tank to your bathrooms. Standard plumbing unions and threads make installation fast and clean for any local plumber.
Does it require regular maintenance, cartridge changes, or power?
None whatsoever! The catalytic alloy core operates passively via hydrodynamic water turbulence. There are no consumable cartridges, no salt to add, no resin beads to wash, and no electrical connection needed. The solid SS304/SS316 housing is built to last 25+ years.
